01Who we are and what this covers

This policy explains what data The Chronicles of History handles: this website, the YouTube channel @chroniclesofhistorytv, the TikTok account @chroniclesofhistorytv and the software that produces and publishes the videos. The project is run by one person, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: open an issue at github.com/adrlopech/Chronicles-of-History/issues or write in the comments of the channel.

02Visitors to this website

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. It loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies: every file is served from this site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to run the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs. Links to YouTube, TikTok and other sites lead to places where their own policies apply.

03Viewers of the videos

YouTube and TikTok handle your viewing data under their own policies (Google, TikTok). From YouTube we only receive the channel’s own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API: views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers gained and traffic sources, as totals per video and per day. These numbers do not identify any viewer and are used only to decide which kinds of stories to make next.

04Comments

If comment replies are enabled, the software reads the public comments on the channel’s own videos and lists the ones without a reply. Only the comment text, the public display name, the video and the date are used, to answer them. Suggested replies are drafted by an AI model from the comment text (without your name) and a person reads and approves each one: nothing is posted automatically. The working list stays on the owner’s computer and is never shared or sold; ask and it will be deleted.

05Google and YouTube API data

The software signs in only to its own Google account and channel. It uses the permissions to upload and schedule the channel’s videos and thumbnails, read the channel’s own videos, and read the channel’s own statistics. It does not access anyone else’s account. The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time in your Google account’s security settings.

06TikTok

Videos are published to the account @chroniclesofhistorytv. We do not collect or store data about TikTok viewers.

07Services used to make the videos

Scripts are drafted with AI language models (Google Gemini) and narrated with a synthetic voice (ElevenLabs). Only the text of the script is sent to them: no personal data of viewers or visitors.

08Keys and security

API keys and sign-in tokens stay in files on the owner’s computer that are excluded from the public source code.

09How long data is kept, and your choices

Statistics are kept as long as they help decide what to make next. You can ask for any comment data about you to be deleted, and you can manage your data on YouTube and TikTok in their own settings.

10Children

The videos are general-audience history. The site and the software collect no data from anyone, including children.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.

12Contact

Contact: open an issue at github.com/adrlopech/Chronicles-of-History/issues or write in the comments of the channel.
