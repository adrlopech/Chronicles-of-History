About

The Chronicles of History is a one-person studio that tells one true story from history every day in a narrated, hand-drawn vertical video of just over a minute, on YouTube and TikTok.
The Chronicles of History is a one-person studio that tells one true story from history every day in a narrated, hand-drawn vertical video of just over a minute, on YouTube and TikTok. Each story is drawn in the look of its own age, from the Stone Age to the Space Age, by software that paints a cast of 127 historical characters, 25 animals and 71 structures. Scripts are drafted with AI language models and checked by an automated fact-check pass before the narration is recorded with a synthetic voice.
