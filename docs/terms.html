01Acceptance of these terms

These terms apply to this website and to the channels YouTube @chroniclesofhistorytv and TikTok @chroniclesofhistorytv (together, “The Chronicles of History”). By reading the site or watching the videos you accept them. If you do not, please do not use them.

02What The Chronicles of History is

A one-person studio that makes short vertical videos (just over a minute) telling real stories from history. Most of the work is automated by software the owner wrote and runs: scripts are drafted by AI language models and checked by an automated fact-check pass; the narration is a synthetic voice; the pictures are drawn by code. There is no sign-up, no user account, nothing to buy and no service for third parties. The software only publishes to the channel’s own accounts.

03The videos are information, not advice

  • Stories simplify. A video of a minute cannot hold every detail. Where historians disagree, the video says so.
  • Illustrations are artistic. Characters, buildings and maps are stylised drawings, not historical photographs or surveys.
  • Mistakes can happen. If you spot an error, write to us and we will correct or remove the video.
  • Not professional advice. Nothing in the videos is legal, medical, financial or academic advice.

04YouTube and TikTok

Names of real people, peoples and places in the stories are used for education and commentary.
